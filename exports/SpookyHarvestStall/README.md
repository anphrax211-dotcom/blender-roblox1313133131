# Spooky Harvest stall: building it in Roblox Studio

`BuildSpookyHarvestStall.lua` builds the Halloween market stall from native Roblox parts. Nothing has to be
uploaded. It's generated by `blender/spooky_stall.py`, which also renders the previews
(`previews/SpookyHarvest_*.png`).

## Option A: FBX import (`SpookyHarvestStall.fbx`)
The FBX holds 111 MeshParts (about 45k triangles). They're merged per section, Roblox material and colour, and
each part's name carries what an FBX can't (`<Section>__<Material>__<RRGGBB>`, plus `__T<transparency %>`,
`__NC` for no collision and `__L<n>` for a light).
1. **Import:** Home or Avatar tab → **Import 3D** → `SpookyHarvestStall.fbx`, with Scale Unit = **Stud**, Merge
   Meshes = **OFF** and Insert Using Scene Position = **ON**.
2. **Set up:** name the model `SpookyHarvestStall`, paste `SpookyHarvest_Setup.lua` into the Command Bar and press
   Enter. It sets Material, Color, Transparency and CanCollide from the names, anchors everything, sorts the parts
   into section folders, and adds the sign text, the 5 soft PointLights and the `ShopInteractZone`.
3. **Prompt:** `AI_PROMPT.md` is a ready-to-send prompt for another chat or the Studio assistant. Attach the FBX,
   the setup script and `fbx_manifest.json`.

Regenerate with `python3 blender/export_spooky_stall.py`.

## Option B: build from native parts (`BuildSpookyHarvestStall.lua`)
1. Open your place in Roblox Studio. Turn on **View → Command Bar**.
2. Open `BuildSpookyHarvestStall.lua`, copy all of it, paste it into the Command Bar and press **Enter**.
3. `Workspace.SpookyHarvestStall` appears at the origin and is selected. Move it with the Move tool, or with
   `workspace.SpookyHarvestStall:PivotTo(CFrame.new(x, y, z))`.

Running it again replaces the old copy. After building, you can right-click the model → **Save to File** to
keep it as a `.rbxm`.

## What's inside (Workspace.SpookyHarvestStall)

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `Structure` | `Base` plinth (the PrimaryPart), `Posts` with iron collars and gold gem finials, `BackWall` planks and battens, `SideWalls`, top `Frame` (headers, beams, plank roof) |
| `Counter` | counter top and lip, `FrontBoards`, `IronStraps` and bolts, `CarvedTrim`, the carved `PlaquePumpkin`, `FrontVines`, `FrontLeaves` |
| `Canopy` | 8 purple/orange `Stripes` (Fabric), `Seams` and hems, `Valance` pennants with diamond cut-outs, `SideDrops`, iron `Supports` |
| `Sign` | `SignBoard` with a SurfaceGui (`SignGui.Title` = "SPOOKY HARVEST", font LuckiestGuy, centred), `Frame`, two purple bats, `CrestPumpkin`, `Vines` |
| `Lanterns` | two `HangingLantern` models under the canopy, two `PumpkinLantern` models on iron brackets |
| `CounterProps` | `BigJackOLantern` and two small pumpkins (player's left), `CandyJarTall` and `CandyJarSmall` (player's right), `TableRunner` with a gold bat, `ServingBoard`, `CounterLeaves` |
| `Decor` | `PostVines`, autumn `Leaves`, two `Cobweb` models |
| `ShopInteractZone` | an invisible, non-colliding box in front of the free middle of the counter. Put a ProximityPrompt or your shop trigger here. |

Each multi-part object (pumpkins, lanterns, bats, jars, leaves, cobwebs) is its own Model, so it can be moved or
deleted in one click.

## Size and settings
- **Size:** about 15 studs wide, 6.5 deep and 15 tall. The counter top is 3.4 studs high, which suits a default
  character. The front of the stall faces −Z.
- **Collision:** structure parts collide. Decorations, the canopy pennants and lights don't (`CanCollide`,
  `CanTouch` off). Everything is Anchored.
- **Materials:**
  - wood is `WoodPlanks` for the planks and `Wood` for the posts and beams;
  - the canopy and runner are `Fabric`;
  - iron is `Metal` and the jars are `Glass`.
- **Glow:** `Neon` is only used on the carved pumpkin faces and lantern glass, in dimmed colours. Five soft
  orange `PointLight`s light the stall (Brightness 0.55–0.7, Range 6–10, no shadows): two lanterns, two pumpkin
  lanterns and the big jack-o'-lantern. Raise `Brightness` on those lights if you want more glow at night.
- **Pumpkins:** each is a core plus 8 lobes made from `SpecialMesh` spheres (MeshType Sphere) on block parts,
  with a curved stem and a curling tendril.
- **Jack-o'-lantern faces:**
  - the eyes and nose are triangles built from pairs of mirrored `WedgePart`s;
  - the grin is a crescent of five segments with pointed corners and two pumpkin-coloured teeth;
  - every glowing cut-out (dimmed Neon) sits on a slightly larger dark-orange "carved rim";
  - the pieces are named `GlowEyeL`, `RimNose`, `GlowGrin3`, `ToothTop` and so on, inside each pumpkin model.

## Editing
- **Sign text:** change `SignGui.Title.Text` on `Sign.SignBoard`, or `SIGN_TEXT` at the top of the script.
- **Colours:** select parts and change `Color`. The canopy stripes are named `Stripe1`–`Stripe8`.
- **Layout:** to regenerate with different measurements, edit `blender/spooky_stall.py`, then run
  `python3 blender/spooky_stall.py` (add `--render previews` for new preview images).
